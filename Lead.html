Lead Follow-Up & CRM Tracker

A lead management system designed to organize prospects, track communication, monitor follow-up dates, and maintain an organized sales pipeline.

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how a Virtual Assistant can maintain an organized lead database and support consistent client follow-up.


Leads are categorized by status and tracked according to their latest interaction and next follow-up action. This helps prevent missed follow-ups and keeps information organized.
