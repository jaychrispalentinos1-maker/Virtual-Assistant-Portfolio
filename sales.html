Sales & Expense Tracker

A spreadsheet-based business tracking system designed to organize transactions, monitor income and expenses, and provide a simple financial overview.

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I organize and manage business transaction data using spreadsheet tools. The system allows transactions to be recorded, categorized, reviewed, and summarized for easier business monitoring.


The dashboard provides a quick overview of income, expenses, net amount, transaction volume, and expense categories.
