Appointment Management System

A scheduling and appointment tracking system designed to organize meetings, monitor appointment status, and help prevent missed or overlapping schedules.

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how a Virtual Assistant can organize appointments and maintain an accurate scheduling system.


The system provides an overview of upcoming appointments, client information, meeting times, appointment status, and scheduling notes.
